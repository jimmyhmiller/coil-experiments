This is the largest and most complete experiment: a compile-time Forth frontend for Coil. It tokenizes raw Forth source, strips both comment forms, sanitizes word names into legal identifiers, and emits ordinary native functions backed by a 16,384-cell data stack, 4,096-cell return stack, and one megabyte of memory. Control structures become native Coil control flow rather than an interpreted continuation mechanism.

Two synchronization primitives built on newer Coil standard-library facilities.
